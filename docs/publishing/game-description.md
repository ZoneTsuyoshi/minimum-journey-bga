## 説明文（Description）

```text
Year 2XXX. A meteor strike pushed humanity to the brink, and the space programs of the old world were lost with it. A century later, civilization has recovered, and a new generation of scientists has begun building exploration ships from the relics their predecessors left behind. The space race has started again.

In MINIMUM JOURNEY, you are an astronaut in charge of your nation's exploration ship. Each round, all players secretly and simultaneously choose to Draw or Stop. Drawing flips the top card of your deck and adds its exploration power to your ship, but be careful: if you are the only player who draws, that card is turned sideways and ignored, and your exploration output is halved for the round.

Once everyone has stopped, players explore in order: those who prepared fewer cards and have less power go first. Spend your exploration output to acquire new cards from the rows of the Solar System, the Milky Way, the Virgo Cluster, and the Laniakea Supercluster. Each card brings fame and a special effect that triggers when drawn, while exploring, or when acquired. At the end of every round, your whole deck is shuffled again.

The game ends in the round when someone reaches the Unknown Region, or when the Laniakea deck runs out. The player with the most fame wins.

Push your luck against your rivals, read their exploration output, and decide how far to go in a race where a small ship can still make a long journey.
```
